SMS Terms

  1. Program description. EmberEye Alerts sends automated safety alert text messages from a customer's EmberEye fire-detection hub to mobile numbers enrolled by the property owner. Messages include possible-fire detections, alert resolutions, system armed/disarmed changes, a one-time enrollment confirmation, and replies to HELP and STOP.
  2. Enrollment. You can enroll by completing the EmberEye Alerts web form and checking the consent box, or by asking the property owner to add your number during EmberEye setup. By enrolling you confirm that you are the subscriber of the mobile number or have the subscriber's permission.
  3. Message frequency. Message frequency varies with activity at the protected property. A typical month has 0–10 messages; an active incident may produce several messages within minutes.
  4. Cost. Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details of your plan. EmberEye does not charge for EmberEye Alerts messages.
  5. Opting out. Reply STOP to any message to cancel. You will receive one confirmation text and no further messages unless you re-enroll. Opting out of alerts does not disable the EmberEye system itself.
  6. Help. Reply HELP to any message, or contact conormccaulley@gmail.com.

Do not rely on EmberEye Alerts as your only means of fire notification. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.
