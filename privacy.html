EmberEye Alerts

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026. This policy covers the EmberEye Alerts SMS program operated by EmberEye ("we", "us").

Who we are

EmberEye makes EmberEye, a fire-detection and alerting system installed at a customer's own property. The EmberEye Alerts SMS program sends safety alert text messages from a customer's EmberEye hub to the mobile numbers that the property owner has enrolled.

What we collect

  • Enrollment information: the name you give, your mobile phone number, and the name or address of the property the EmberEye system protects.
  • Consent record: the date, time and method by which you opted in (for example, the enrollment web form), and any STOP or HELP replies you send.
  • Message log: the alert messages sent to your number, their delivery status, and the time they were sent.

We do not collect the content of any other text messages, your contacts, or your location through this program.

How we use it

  • To send you the alerts you enrolled for: possible-fire detections, alert resolutions, and system armed/disarmed status changes for your property.
  • To send a one-time enrollment confirmation and to respond to HELP and STOP requests.
  • To diagnose delivery problems and to keep a record of consent as required by carriers and regulators.

We do not use your phone number for marketing. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Who we share it with

Messages are delivered through Twilio Inc., our SMS service provider, which processes your phone number and message content solely to deliver the messages. Mobile carriers receive the messages in order to deliver them. We do not share your information with any other third party except as required by law.

Where your data lives

Your enrolled number and the message log are stored on the EmberEye hub installed at the protected property, under the control of the property owner. Our SMS provider retains message records according to its own retention policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your enrollment information for as long as your number is enrolled, and keep the consent and opt-out record for the period required by applicable regulations after you unsubscribe.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving alerts. You will receive one final confirmation text.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us at conormccaulley@gmail.com.
  • To correct or delete your enrollment information, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do. Material changes will be communicated by text to enrolled numbers.
